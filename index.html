Trading Psychology & Risk Management

Emotional stability is the cornerstone of professional trading. Research indicates that over 90% of retail traders fail due to emotional biases rather than market dynamics. Main cognitive biases include:

  • FOMO (Fear Of Missing Out): Entering trades without setup confluence because of a fast-moving price.
  • Revenge Trading: Attempting to recoup losses by immediately entering larger, unplanned positions.
  • Overtrading: Over-allocating capital or placing too many trades, leading to elevated transaction fees and mental fatigue.

Data Quality & Machine Learning Readiness

High-quality data is indispensable for predictive modeling. Prior to feeding datasets into machine learning algorithms, engineers must undergo extensive clean-up checks:

  • Null & Missing Values: Handled by imputation (mean, median, mode) or line deletion to prevent model bias.
  • Statistical Outliers: Identified via Z-Score or IQR methods to avoid skewing regression coefficients.
  • Feature Correlation: High multicollinearity can distort model interpretability. Correlation matrices are essential.
