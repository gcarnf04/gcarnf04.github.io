Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 23, 2026

At AI & Data Tools, accessible from our suite of applications, one of our main priorities is the privacy of our visitors. This Privacy Policy document contains types of information that is collected and recorded by AI & Data Tools and how we use it.

1. Client-Side Only Processing

We pride ourselves on a zero-server data ingestion architecture. All data file parsing (CSVs, TSVs, logs) is done strictly within your browser using client-side technologies (e.g. PapaParse). Your raw datasets are never uploaded, sent, saved, or processed on our servers.

2. Bring Your Own Key (BYOK) Encryption

Our applications require a Gemini API Key to perform advanced AI-powered diagnostics. Your API Key is handled with high security measures:

  • Local Encryption: Your key is encrypted in your browser using AES-256 (via CryptoJS) using a custom 4-digit PIN that only you know.
  • Local Storage: The encrypted key is stored exclusively in your browser's local storage (localStorage) to share it securely across tools of the same origin.
  • Direct Call: Requests are initiated directly from your browser to the official Google Gemini API endpoint. There are no intermediary third-party backend servers.

3. Information We Collect

If you contact us directly, we may receive additional information about you such as your name, email address, the contents of the message and/or attachments you may send us, and any other information you may choose to provide.

4. Google AdSense & Third-Party Cookies

We use Google AdSense to serve advertisements on our web tools. Google AdSense utilizes cookies to serve ads based on a user's prior visits to our website or other websites on the Internet.

  • Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to our users based on their visit to our sites and/or other sites on the Internet.
  • Users may opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Ads Settings or by utilizing general cookie opt-out tools.

5. Log Files

AI & Data Tools follows a standard procedure of using log files. These files log visitors when they visit websites. All hosting companies do this as part of hosting services' analytics. The information collected by log files includes internet protocol (IP) addresses, browser type, Internet Service Provider (ISP), date and time stamp, referring/exit pages, and possibly the number of clicks. These are not linked to any information that is personally identifiable.

6. Consent

By using our website, you hereby consent to our Privacy Policy and agree to its terms.
