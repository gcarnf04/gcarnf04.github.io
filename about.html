About Us & Contact

Our Mission

At AI & Data Tools, our mission is to build private, serverless, and powerful utility applications that put the power of Artificial Intelligence directly in the user's hands. We believe that professional-grade analytics and auditing should not require uploading sensitive files to external corporate servers.

Why Serverless?

Traditional SaaS platforms process your files on their databases. In contrast, our tools parse your datasets locally inside your own browser tab. By leveraging Google's Gemini public APIs in a Bring Your Own Key (BYOK) paradigm, we enable state-of-the-art diagnostics while maintaining a zero-server footprint. Your data remains exactly where it belongs: on your machine.

Who We Are

We are a dedicated team of software engineers, quantitative traders, and data architects committed to transparency, open-source mechanics, and privacy-first web design. We leverage Harmony and Vanilla CSS to ensure our applications load instantly and look premium.
